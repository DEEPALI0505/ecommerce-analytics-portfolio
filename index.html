Eliminate Blanket Sitewide Discounts

Finding: Welch's t-test confirmed no statistically significant AOV lift from discounts ($139.86 vs $143.68, p = 0.223), but discounts eroded gross margins by 5.2 percentage points.

Action: Replace universal 10–20% promo codes with threshold-based promotions (e.g., "Spend $175, Get Free Expedited Shipping") to protect margins while driving basket size above the $142 AOV mean.
2

Automate Day-35 Re-Engagement Cadence

Finding: First-to-second order churn is 55.75%, and the average inter-purchase cycle for repeat buyers is 46.5 days.

Action: Launch an automated 3-part email/SMS sequence starting at Day 35 post-purchase offering replenishment or complementary item suggestions before the 60-day churn cliff.
3

Drive First-Order Basket Bundling

Finding: Customers who add 2+ items on Order 1 generate +39.9% higher 2-year LTV ($371.31 vs $265.36, p = 3.12e-66).

Action: Deploy "Frequently Bought Together" bundles on product pages (e.g. Espresso Machine + Grinder with 20.3% affinity) with 1-click cart addition.
4

Win Back At-Risk Champions ($278K Pool)

Finding: 754 customers (19.6%) are categorized as "At Risk", holding over $278,000 in past spend with recency > 500 days.

Action: Deploy a dedicated high-touch re-activation flow (VIP survey + personalized credit) to recover 5-10% of this dormant revenue pool ($14K-$28K immediate lift).
